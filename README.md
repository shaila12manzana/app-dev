# My Favorite Series: Steven Universe

## About the Series

**Steven Universe** is an animated fantasy series created by **Rebecca Sugar**. It follows *Steven*, a kind-hearted half-human, half-Gem boy who lives in Beach City with the **Crystal Gems**, a team of magical warriors who protect Earth.

> "If every pork chop were perfect, we wouldn't have hot dogs."
